# Pixelcraft philosophy

## Recognise the pattern, not the author

AI slop is a quality description, not a reliable provenance label. The toolkit examines visible and implemented patterns, documents evidence, and avoids unsupported claims about authorship.

## Specificity beats decoration

A screen should reflect its product, user, data, and workflow. Generic decoration cannot substitute for product meaning.

## Preserve before replacing

Keep effective brand assets, genuine content, and working behavior. Change only what has an observable reason to change.

## Truth is a design property

Fake metrics, unverified testimonials, invented integrations, and fabricated UI data damage trust. Visual polish must not make unsupported claims more persuasive.

## Behaviour is part of the interface

A polished static view is not a finished product. Loading, empty, error, disabled, focus, success, and responsive states are part of the design.

## Evidence over certainty

Every finding should identify what was observed, why it matters, how confident the reviewer is, and what else could explain it.
